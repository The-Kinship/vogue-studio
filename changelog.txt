0.2.0: Drag to bend! Press a joint's circle and drag around it: the body part turns to follow your mouse, like a paper doll, and Brio holds the pose. Undo, Redo and Reset pose buttons. Simple mode (on by default) shows only the main joints so the chest isn't crowded; names now show on hover and on the selected part (you can turn all names back on). Drag strength setting for finer bending.

Click a circle or a name in the list to select that body part. Settings: circle size, show names, hide circles. Status lines say in words if something's missing (not in GPose, Brio not answering).
